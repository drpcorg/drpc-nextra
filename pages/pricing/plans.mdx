# Plans and billing

dRPC charges for usage in Compute Units (CU). 1M CU costs $0.30 on every plan. Plans differ in how many CU each request uses, so the price per request is lower on Pro and Scale.

## Plans

|                                   | Free              | Pay as You Go      | Pro                      | Scale                  | Enterprise    |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------ | ---------------------- | ------------- |
| Price                             | $0                | $6 per 1M requests | $49/mo                   | $199/mo                | Custom        |
| Included CU                       | 210M CU per month | —                  | 163M CU per month        | 664M CU per month      | Custom        |
| Multi-Chain requests              | ~10M              | Pay per request    | ~10.2M,<br />$4.8 per 1M | ~47M,<br />$4.2 per 1M | Custom        |
| Solana requests                   | —                 | Pay per request    | ~12.5M,<br/>$3.9 per 1M  | ~51M,<br/>$3.9 per 1M  | Custom        |
| Rate limit                        | Public limits     | 100 RPS            | 300 RPS                  | 600 RPS                | Custom        |
| Nodes                             | Public nodes      | Premium nodes      | Premium nodes            | Premium nodes          | Premium nodes |
| Archive, Debug & Trace, WebSocket | No                | Yes                | Yes                      | Yes                    | Yes           |
| Solana gRPC                       | No                | Add-on             | $60/TB, included         | $60/TB, included       | Custom        |

The included CU are one shared pool: you can spend them on Multi-Chain and Solana in any mix.
Rate limits are set in CU per second. Heavier methods use more CU, so the request rate for them is lower.

## How requests use CU

|                     | Pay as You Go     | Pro                       | Scale                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Multi-Chain request | 20 CU ($6 per 1M) | 16 CU ($4.8 per 1M)       | 14 CU ($4.2 per 1M)       |
| Solana request      | 20 CU ($6 per 1M) | 13 CU ($3.9 per 1M)       | 13 CU ($3.9 per 1M)       |
| Solana gRPC         | Add-on            | 200M CU per 1 TB ($60/TB) | 200M CU per 1 TB ($60/TB) |

WebSocket subscriptions and notifications use the same CU as a regular request on your plan. Batch calls count each request inside the batch (5 batched = 5 requests). See Compute units for the full method list.

## Billing cycle

Plans are paid from your dRPC balance and renew on the 1st of each month.

- **First month is prorated.** You pay and get CU only for the days left until the 1st. Example: Pro bought on October 19 costs ~$21 and includes ~69M CU for October 19–31.
- **From the 1st, the full cycle.** On November 1 the plan renews for $49 with the full 163M CU, and the same on the 1st of every next month.
- **Unused CU don't carry over.** The pool resets on the 1st.

## When your plan's CU run out

Your requests keep working. Usage beyond the pool is charged from your balance: 20 CU per Multi-Chain request ($6 per 1M) and 13 CU per Solana request ($3.9 per 1M).

Example: on Pro you use the whole pool by October 25 and make 2M more Multi-Chain requests by the end of the month. 2M × $6 per 1M = $12 is charged from your balance.

## Your dRPC balance

- **What it pays for:** plan payments, Pay as You Go usage and usage beyond your plan.
- **How to top up:** by card (from $49) or crypto (from $10). The balance doesn't expire.
- **Alerts:** we email you when the balance drops below $100 and $50. You can change the thresholds in Billing → Balance Alerts.

## If your balance runs out

- If you still have CU left in your plan, requests continue as normal.
- If the plan's CU are used up and the balance is empty, requests stop until you top up.
- Paid accounts don't fall back to the Free plan.

## If your renewal fails

If there isn't enough balance on the 1st, your plan is suspended and you get a 3-day grace period. Top up within the grace period and the plan renews automatically. If you don't, the plan is cancelled and your account moves to Pay as You Go.

## Cancel or change your plan

- **Cancel** anytime in Billing. Your plan stays active until the end of the month and won't renew. Changed your mind? Resume it before the month ends at no extra cost.
- **After a plan ends**, your account moves to Pay as You Go.
- **To switch between Pro and Scale**, contact support.

## Track your usage

- **Plan pool and reset date:** Billing → Usage & Savings.
- **Spending and payment history:** Billing → Overview.
- **Usage by network and method:** Stats.

## Accounts created before October 20, 2026

Nothing changes unless you buy a plan: your current terms and rate limits stay the same. Plans are optional. Buying a plan moves your account to the new billing permanently. When the plan ends, your account moves to Pay as You Go with a 100 RPS limit, not back to your current terms.

## Enterprise

Custom volume pricing, unlimited RPS, committed SLAs, SSO, dedicated clusters and dedicated support. Enterprise terms are set by contract, contact sales to discuss them.
