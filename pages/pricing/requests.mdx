# Plans

dRPC has five options: Free, Pay as You Go, Pro, Scale and Enterprise.

- **Free**: public nodes only, 210M CU per 30 days, rate limits apply.
- **Pay as You Go**: \$6 per 1M requests (20 CU per request at \$0.30 per 1M CU), no commitment. New accounts are limited to 100 RPS.
- **Pro (\$49/mo) and Scale (\$199/mo)**: a monthly CU pool (163M and 664M CU) at a lower price per request, 300 and 600 RPS. Usage beyond the pool is charged from your dRPC balance at Pay as You Go rates. Plans are paid from your balance: the first month is prorated, then billed on the 1st of each month. Unused CU don't carry over.
- **Enterprise**: custom terms, contact sales.
  Existing accounts keep their current terms. Full details: [drpc.org/pricing](https://drpc.org/pricing)
